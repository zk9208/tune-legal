Privacy Policy

01. About Tune

Tune is an independent private music player published and maintained by the Tune developer identified in its Google Play listing. You can choose audio on your device or connect to a music server you manage or are authorized to access. Tune does not supply music, operate your music server, provide a music download catalog or relay your audio through a developer-operated server.

02. Music and local app data

On first launch, Tune requests music and audio access on Android 13 or later, or read access to shared storage on Android 12 and earlier. It uses this permission only to read audio information from Android’s media index, display available music folders and play music. Each device-source card stores one selected folder; choosing another folder replaces that selection and its library index. Tune imports the audio indexed by Android in the selected folder and its subfolders. Existing read-only folder grants from older versions remain supported. File addresses, song titles, artists, albums, duration and embedded artwork may be indexed or cached on your device. Source selections, playback queues, language, player appearance, spectrum styles and sleep-timer settings are also stored locally. Your local music and its index are not uploaded to the developer or other servers. Tune does not read your photos or videos, request all-files access, or request permission to write to shared storage.

03. Connections to your servers

When you test, save or use a connection, the address, username, password, API key or access token you supply, a generated client/device identifier when required, and library or playback requests are sent directly to the server you specify. A one-time verification code is used for the current sign-in and is not saved locally. Library information and artwork returned by the server may be cached on your device. The server operator determines its own data practices. Tune supports compatible Synology Audio Station, OpenSubsonic, Jellyfin, Emby, Polaris and Logitech/Lyrion Media Server interfaces and is not affiliated with or endorsed by those providers.

04. Purchases and the free trial

Google Play processes Tune Pro payments and returns purchase and entitlement status to the app. Tune does not receive card numbers or full payment credentials. Google may process device, purchase and diagnostic information under its own privacy policy. Only when you start the free 7-day trial, Tune stores its start time and the most recently observed device time locally. The trial requires no payment method and never charges automatically. Android backup, if enabled, may back up and restore only this trial timing record through your Google account. The Tune developer cannot access the backup. Backup is not a reliable guarantee against restarting a trial after reinstalling.

05. Purpose, storage and security

App data is used to import and display your music, authenticate to your selected servers, synchronize libraries, play audio, retain preferences and manage Pro access. Server credentials are encrypted on your device with Android Keystore protection. Other library and preference data remains in app-local storage. Credentials, music, library indexes, artwork and other settings are excluded from Android backup; only the trial timing record is eligible. Tune has no developer-operated advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or music-relay service, does not sell your personal data and does not use it for targeted advertising. The developer does not receive your music library or server credentials through the app.

06. Your network choices

You may configure HTTP or HTTPS for a server. HTTP sends credentials, verification codes and playback requests without encryption and exposes them to interception or modification on the network. HTTPS validates server certificates. Prefer HTTPS, protect your accounts and use only servers and networks you trust. These choices do not remove the developer’s security or data-protection duties under applicable law.

If an HTTPS server omits an intermediate certificate, Tune may download publicly available issuer certificates from the authority-information-access (AIA) addresses in its certificate chain and temporarily cache them on the device. These separate requests contain no NAS login credentials, access tokens or music contents. The download service can receive normal network information, including the connecting IP address, request time and requested certificate address, under its own data practices. Downloaded certificates must still pass system certificate verification; downloading them does not make an issuer trusted.

For an otherwise valid private or unrecognized server certificate, Tune may ask you to compare its SHA-256 fingerprint directly with your server settings before approving it. An approval stores the server address, port, exact certificate and fingerprint locally, outside Android backup. It applies only to that server and certificate, and does not add a device-wide certificate authority. Certificate changes require new approval; expired certificates and hostname mismatches remain blocked. You can review and remove these approvals in Tune settings. Clearing app storage or uninstalling Tune also removes them.

07. Retention, removal and your rights

Removing a server source deletes its saved credentials and synchronized tracks from Tune. Removing the device source deletes its local song index and saved folder selection, and releases any retained folder grant from an older version; it does not delete your original music files. Removing a source does not revoke Android’s audio or storage permission. You can revoke that permission in Android Settings. Other app-local data remains until you clear Tune’s storage in Android Settings or uninstall it. Android may restore a backed-up trial record after reinstalling; manage backups in Android/Google settings. Tune does not create Tune user accounts. Contact the relevant server operator to delete an account or data on that server. Clearing Tune does not erase third-party server content or Google Play purchase records. To request access, correction, deletion or other rights available under your local law, use the contact email below.
